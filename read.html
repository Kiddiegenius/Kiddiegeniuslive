Reading Corner

Pick a story level and practice understanding what you read.

Picture Story (Pre-K)

Mia planted a tiny seed. She gave it water and sunshine every day. Soon a little green sprout popped up. Mia smiled and said, “Growing takes time and care.”

Think: What helped the seed grow?

Adventure Story (1st Grade)

Jay built a paper bridge for his toy car. The first bridge bent, so he tried a stronger fold and tested it again. His new bridge held steady. Jay learned that trying again helps ideas get better.

Think: What did Jay do when his first idea failed?

Discovery Story (2nd Grade)

Nia and Leo measured rain in a cup each morning for one week. They made a chart and noticed Thursday had the most rain. They compared numbers and explained what the weather was like.

Think: How did Nia and Leo use data to learn about weather?
